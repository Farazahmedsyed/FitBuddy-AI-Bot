FitBuddy uses an FAQ knowledge file in Botpress to answer practical questions. Here are examples of the details it can explain.

01 · HOURS

Plan your visit

Open Monday–Friday, 6 AM–10 PM, and Saturday–Sunday, 8 AM–8 PM. Ask the front desk about holiday hours.

02 · MEMBERSHIPS

Find your fit

Student: $20/month with a valid student ID. Basic: $30/month. Premium: $45/month with scheduled group classes.

03 · CLASSES

Move together

Beginner-friendly 45-minute cardio, strength, and mobility classes run throughout the week. Ask FitBuddy for the timetable.

How to use it

Answers grounded in the FAQ.

Ask about membership prices and benefits, class times, or equipment. FitBuddy draws on the faq.md knowledge file in Botpress and shows a source alongside its answers.

Helpful things to know

  • Cardio: Monday and Wednesday at 6 PM; strength: Tuesday and Thursday at 6 PM.
  • Mobility: Saturday at 10 AM. Reserve a class spot at the front desk at least two hours beforehand.
  • Facilities include cardio machines, free weights, strength machines, lockers, and showers.

FitBuddy provides information but cannot make class reservations. Check with the front desk for holiday hours or changes to availability.
